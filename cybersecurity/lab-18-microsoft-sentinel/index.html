CYBERSECURITY LAB 18

Microsoft Sentinel

Querying Azure activity and storage telemetry with Microsoft Sentinel and KQL.

Lab Overview

Microsoft Sentinel was enabled on the existing Lab 17 Log Analytics workspace. Azure Activity Log and storage telemetry were queried with KQL to establish baselines and investigate controlled activity. Defender portal onboarding permissions prevented validating analytics rules, incidents, workbooks, and automation.

Areas of Focus

  • Sentinel setup and validation of available data connectors
  • AzureActivity and StorageBlobLogs queries using KQL
  • Documented scope limits where Defender onboarding permissions were unavailable
