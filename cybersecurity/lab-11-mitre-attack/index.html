CYBERSECURITY LAB 11

MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

Connecting observed behaviors and security telemetry to adversary tactics and techniques.

Lab Overview

This lab uses the MITRE ATT&CK knowledge base to describe and contextualize observed behavior. Structured mapping helps communicate investigative findings and identify defensive visibility.

Areas of Focus

  • Adversary tactics and techniques
  • Mapping observations to ATT&CK behavior
  • Using ATT&CK to communicate coverage and gaps
