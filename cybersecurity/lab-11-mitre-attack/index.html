CYBERSECURITY LAB 11

MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

Mapping observed lab activity to MITRE ATT&CK with evidence-based reasoning.

Lab Overview

This lab maps activities documented in earlier exercises to MITRE ATT&CK based on collected evidence. It distinguishes observed behavior from inferred intent and attempted actions from confirmed outcomes; a tool name alone is not treated as proof of a technique.

Areas of Focus

  • Describing behavior rather than simply naming tools
  • Grounding technique mappings in observable evidence
  • Recording uncertainty and differentiating attempts from outcomes
