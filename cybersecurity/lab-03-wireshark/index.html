CYBERSECURITY LAB 03

Network Traffic Analysis

Capturing and analyzing network protocols and connection behavior with Wireshark.

Lab Overview

This lab used Wireshark to examine traffic generated between systems on the isolated Host-Only network. Analysis covered common protocols and TCP behavior, with a comparison of plaintext application traffic and encrypted SSH communication.

Areas of Focus

  • ARP, ICMP, DNS, TCP, HTTP, FTP, and SSH traffic
  • TCP connection establishment and teardown
  • Differences between plaintext and encrypted communication
