CYBERSECURITY LAB 12

Detection Engineering

Turning observable activity into testable, useful security detections.

Lab Overview

Three custom Suricata detections were developed in Security Onion and tested against controlled Nmap service discovery, FTP password-guessing activity, and access to /etc/passwd. The workflow included validating alerts and tuning FTP alert volume to improve signal quality.

Areas of Focus

  • Translating observed behavior into custom Suricata rules
  • Validating Nmap, FTP, and file-access detections against lab traffic
  • Tuning alert volume and reviewing detection results
