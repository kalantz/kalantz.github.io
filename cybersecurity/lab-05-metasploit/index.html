CYBERSECURITY LAB 05

Metasploit Framework

Validating a known vulnerability and performing controlled post-exploitation enumeration.

Lab Overview

Building on earlier reconnaissance and vulnerability scanning, this lab used Metasploit to validate and exploit the known vsFTPd 2.3.4 vulnerability on the isolated Metasploitable 2 target. The resulting Meterpreter session was verified, used for limited system enumeration, and then terminated.

Areas of Focus

  • Validating the identified service vulnerability
  • Establishing and verifying a Meterpreter session
  • Controlled account, system, process, and network enumeration
