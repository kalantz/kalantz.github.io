CYBERSECURITY LAB 04

Vulnerability Scanning

Assessing scanner findings and independently validating selected issues in an isolated lab.

Lab Overview

Nessus was used to scan the intentionally vulnerable Metasploitable 2 system. Findings were reviewed and selected issues were independently validated with tools including Nmap, Netcat, Nping, and VNC Viewer. Testing remained within the isolated Host-Only network.

Areas of Focus

  • Vulnerability discovery and severity review with Nessus
  • Correlating findings with previously identified services
  • Independent validation without unnecessary exploitation
