CYBERSECURITY LAB 02

Network Discovery with Nmap

Discovering hosts and assessing exposed services within the isolated home lab.

Lab Overview

Using Nmap on the isolated Host-Only network, this lab identified active hosts, enumerated ports and services, fingerprinted the target operating system, and checked for potential vulnerabilities. The work focused on reconnaissance and analysis; no exploitation was performed.

Areas of Focus

  • Host discovery and TCP/UDP port scanning
  • Service-version detection and OS fingerprinting
  • Nmap scripting and potential vulnerability discovery
